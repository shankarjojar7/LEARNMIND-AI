# LEARNMIND AI — Pitch

## 30-second pitch
Students do not need another content library. They need a teacher that adapts to them. LearnMind turns their own notes into personalized explanations, practice quizzes, study plans, and learning insights. Amazon Bedrock powers the generative tutor; optional AWS storage supports learning materials and progress.

## Problem
Students have static notes but no personalized explanation, discover gaps late, and spend time creating quizzes and study plans. Language and skill-level mismatches make learning harder.

## Solution
1. Context-aware tutor grounded in uploaded notes
2. Adaptive quiz generator
3. Goal-based study planner
4. Progress and weak-topic insights

## Innovation
A measurable learning loop: **learn → practice → measure → adapt**. The system adapts level, language, and teaching style and prefers the student's own material as its source.

## AWS
Bedrock Converse for generation; S3 for optional material storage; DynamoDB for optional learning events. Production extension: API Gateway, Lambda, Cognito, CloudWatch.

## Business model
Freemium student plan; Pro for larger study libraries and analytics; institutional licenses for colleges/coaching centers; LMS integrations.

## Impact
Accessible explanations, more active recall, realistic study plans, and earlier visibility into learning gaps.

## Closing
LearnMind does not replace teachers. It gives every learner a patient study partner.
